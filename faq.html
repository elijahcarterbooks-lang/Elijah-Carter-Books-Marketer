Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions.

Can you guarantee sales, rankings or reviews?

No. No honest marketer can. I focus on the factors I can influence, and I report on what was delivered.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope. We agree a timeline in your proposal before work begins.

What do I need to start?

Your book details and goals. A cover and any existing links help, but you can start by sending me a message.

Do you work with authors outside the UK and US?

Yes. I support authors worldwide, including Canada and Australia.
